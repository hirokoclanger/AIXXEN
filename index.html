The Platform

A single source of truth for every AI system you operate.

From use-case intake through retirement, AIXXEN classifies, scores, monitors, and evidences every AI system in your enterprise wrapping LLMs, classical ML, computer vision, and agentic & RAG-based systems with the controls regulators now expect.

01 · Classification

Every use case mapped to the four EU AI Act risk tiers.

Intake any AI system and AIXXEN classifies it across all four EU AI Act risk tiers with automatic detection and hard blocking of prohibited AI practices before they enter production.

02 · Risk Engine

A weighted 0–100 risk score with drift detection.

Five-dimension scoring across data quality, model robustness, human oversight, transparency, and societal impact. Trend analysis and drift signals trigger automated mitigation recommendations.

03 · Lifecycle

Risk Methodology

Classification you can defend. Scoring you can explain.

AIXXEN classifies enterprise AI use cases across all four EU AI Act risk tiers, with automatic detection and hard blocking of prohibited AI practices. Around that, a weighted risk scoring engine produces a composite 0–100 score with trend analysis, drift signals, and automated mitigation recommendations.

Prohibited

Banned at intake

Practices the AI Act prohibits are detected and hard-blocked before a use case can be registered.

High Risk

Full controls suite

Conformity assessment templates, technical documentation, human oversight workflows, and post-market monitoring.

Limited Risk

Transparency obligations

Disclosure controls, AI-generated content labelling, and user notification standards enforced on deployment.

Minimal Risk

Lightweight registration

Captured in the inventory with proportional controls visible, but not over-governed.

5
Weighted dimensions

Each AI system is evaluated across five dimensions and rolled up into a composite 0–100 score. Weights are configurable per use case so a customer-facing classifier and an internal forecasting model are not scored the same way.

Model Lifecycle

From intake to retirement, captured by design.

Every model registration, evaluation, incident, and change is captured in an append-only audit log with digital signatures across 17 event types giving auditors the regulatory provenance trail the AI Act expects.

01

Intake

Use case captured, classified against the four EU AI Act risk tiers, and routed to the right governance track.

02

Registration

Model registered with owner, purpose, datasets, deployment surface, and weighted scoring profile.

03

Evaluation

Recurring and event-driven evaluation bias, robustness, drift, and human oversight signals re-scored.

04

Operation

Incidents, change requests, and retraining are tracked, signed, and pushed to ITSM and GRC systems.

05

Retirement

Models are retired through a governed workflow with final attestation and an immutable closing record.

Audit & Provenance

An append-only record. Digitally signed. Always exportable.

17 signed event types span every governance-relevant action across the platform. Every entry is hash-chained, timestamped, and survives the people who created it exactly what auditors and regulators expect to see.

Built for the regulators you actually face.

The audit subsystem is designed to answer the questions your internal audit, conformity assessor, and supervisory authority will ask without a scramble. Provenance is a property of the record, not of the report.
