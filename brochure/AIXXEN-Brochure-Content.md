## Page 3 — The Problem (light)

- **Section number / kicker:** 01 — The Problem
- **Headline:** Your AI is optimized. Your employees aren't.
- **Body:**
  - AI tools are bought faster than any software category before them. Licenses land on departmental cards, pilots multiply, and the invoices stack up across a dozen vendors that no single person reconciles.
  - The benefit side stays unmeasured. Most AI budgets are still justified on projected savings. You know the line. "Five minutes per task across two hundred people." Almost nobody checks it against the P&L afterward.

- **Caption:** Business case analytics in AIXXEN. Projected potential is pulled back to realized value.

  - **30%+** — of GenAI projects abandoned after proof of concept. *Source: Gartner, 2024.*
  - **37%** — of firms have a policy to manage shadow AI. *Source: IBM, Cost of a Data Breach, 2025.*
- **Closing line:** A gap has opened between what AI is assumed to deliver, and what it has delivered.

## Page 4 — The Evidence (dark)

- **Kicker:** 02 — The Evidence
- **Headline:** Adoption raced ahead of accountability.
- **Hero stats:**
  - **95%** — of enterprise generative-AI pilots show no measurable P&L impact. *Source: MIT NANDA, The GenAI Divide, 2025.*
  - **5%** — of companies capture AI value at scale. 60% see none at all. *Source: BCG, The Widening AI Value Gap, 2025.*
  - **88%** — use AI, yet only about 6% see meaningful EBIT impact. *Source: McKinsey, The State of AI, 2025.*
  - **1 in 5** — organizations qualify as AI ROI Leaders that measure return. *Source: Deloitte, AI ROI: The Paradox, 2025.*
- **Synthesis:** Three independent methodologies, one finding. The constraint is not the technology. It is the absence of measurement, governance and a hard-versus-soft discipline. That gap is what AIXXEN closes.

## Page 16 — Questions & Answers (light)

- **Kicker:** 14 — Questions & Answers
- **Headline:** Sharp questions, sharp answers.
- **FAQ:**
  - **How do you calculate ROI on AI investments?** AI ROI is the realized financial benefit minus the total cost of ownership, divided by that cost. AIXXEN calculates it per use case, so you see which AI tools clear the bar and which do not.
  - **What is AI governance, and how does AIXXEN support it?** It is the set of controls that keep AI usage visible, accountable and aligned with policy. AIXXEN provides the inventory behind it, every tool with its spend, owner and measured ROI on one screen.
  - **How do you measure the benefits of AI correctly?** By separating projected potential from realized savings. AIXXEN tracks benefits against a baseline and counts only realized value.
  - **Is AIXXEN vendor-locked?** No. AIXXEN is vendor-neutral by design. Every AI tool you pay for sits on the same page, regardless of vendor.
  - **How quickly can we be live?** Onboarding is small and pragmatic. CSV and Excel import work today. Direct API import is on the roadmap.
