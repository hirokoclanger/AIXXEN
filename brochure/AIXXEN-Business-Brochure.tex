\faq{How do you calculate ROI on AI investments?}{AI ROI is the realized financial benefit minus the total cost of ownership, divided by that cost. The benefit side counts hours saved at fully-loaded labor cost, revenue uplift and reduced error or rework. The cost side counts licenses, infrastructure, integration, training and oversight. AIXXEN calculates this per use case, so you see which AI tools clear the bar and which do not.}

\faq{What is AI governance, and how does AIXXEN support it?}{AI governance is the set of controls that keep an organization's AI usage visible, accountable and aligned with policy. AIXXEN provides the inventory behind it. Every AI tool the organization pays for, with its spend, owner and measured ROI, on one screen. Governance then rests on current evidence rather than a stale spreadsheet.}

\faq{How do you measure the benefits of AI correctly?}{By separating projected potential from realized savings. A pilot that could save 2,000 hours has saved nothing until those hours are reallocated or removed from the cost base. AIXXEN tracks benefits against a baseline, counts only realized value, and flags use cases where the business case and the actual numbers have drifted apart.}

\faq{Is AIXXEN vendor-locked?}{No. AIXXEN is vendor-neutral by design. Every AI tool the organization pays for sits on the same page, regardless of category and regardless of vendor. Bring your own stack.}

\faq{How quickly can we be live?}{Onboarding is small and pragmatic. Once you can hand over your tools, spend, headcount and use-case data, the screens populate. CSV and Excel import work today. Direct API import is on the roadmap.}
